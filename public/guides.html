Guides

Expandable guides for new players and competitive modes.

📖 Getting Started

Launch Minecraft Java, add play.evaulxmc.net, join the Hub, then use the compass or portals to choose a mode.

⚔️ Practice PvP

Work on aim, sprint reset timing, pot management, spacing and predictable strafe breaking. Use unranked before ranked.

💀 UHC Strategy

Mine safely, prioritise iron, diamonds, apples, water and lava. Enchant before fighting and track border movement.

🏹 Survival Games Tips

Learn chest routes, avoid bad early fights, craft when possible and watch refill timers.

🗡️ KitPvP Progression

Earn coins from kills, streaks and bounties. Unlock stronger kits and learn each kit's strengths.

💰 Earning Coins & Ranks

Win games, complete challenges, vote daily and join events for faster rewards. Paid ranks should stay cosmetic and non-pay-to-win.
